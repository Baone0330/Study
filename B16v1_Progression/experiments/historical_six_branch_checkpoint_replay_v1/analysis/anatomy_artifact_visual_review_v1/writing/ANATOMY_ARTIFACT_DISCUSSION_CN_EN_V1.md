# ANATOMY ARTIFACT DISCUSSION CN EN V1.md

The earlier natural-image feature distances were lower for ON, whereas this audit found more line/cross detector responses for ON. The two findings are not contradictory measurements of the same clinical endpoint: feature distributions, geometric candidates, anatomical visibility and clinical utility are separate questions. Neither feature proximity nor a technically significant paired change establishes thyroid tissue authenticity.

上轮 ON 的自然图像特征距离较小，本轮 ON 的直线/十字检测器响应更多；两者不是对同一临床终点的矛盾测量。特征分布、几何候选、解剖可视性与临床效用是不同问题；特征接近或技术配对差异均不证明甲状腺组织真实性。

Candidates may correspond to genuine ultrasound interfaces, scanner rulers/text/UI, acquisition geometry or generated linear structures. Without actual local viewing these are competing explanations, not observed case classifications. OFF has fewer algorithmic candidates, but we cannot claim it looks more natural or has more identifiable thyroid anatomy. No annotated thyroid ROI or target-year ground truth was available; no external segmentation model was introduced.

候选可能来自真实超声界面、标尺/文字/UI、采集几何或生成线性结构；无人实际本地查看时，这些只是待检验解释，不是已观察分类。OFF 算法候选较少，但不能声称其更自然或甲状腺更可辨。缺少甲状腺 ROI 标注和目标年份真值，未引入外部分割模型。

Resampling, nonclinical approximate intersections, region overlap, differing native view/exam support and nonuniform case sampling constrain interpretation. The fixed48-case set is for future error analysis, not a population prevalence estimate. No human reference means sensitivity, specificity, false-positive rates, Kappa and ICC are unknown rather than zero. Subsequent qualified local review, PHI clearance and an independently justified scale/ROI reference are prerequisites for medical conclusions.

重采样、非临床近似交点、区域重叠、不同原生 view/Exam 支持以及非均匀抽样限制解释。固定48对用于后续错误分析，不是总体发生率估计。无人参考时灵敏度、特异度、假阳性率、Kappa、ICC 为未知而非0。真实合规本地医生复核、PHI 核准及独立论证的量表/ROI 参考是医学结论的前提。
